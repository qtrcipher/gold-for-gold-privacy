عند تحديث الأسعار

يطلب التطبيق أسعار الذهب والفضة من Gold API وأسعار الصرف مقابل الدولار من ExchangeRate-API عبر اتصال مشفّر. يتلقى المزودان عنوان الإنترنت (IP) الذي صدر منه الطلب. لا تتضمن الطلبات عملتك المختارة أو أوزان مقتنياتك أو نتائج حساباتك أو معرّف جهازك.

قد يسجّل Gold API مسار الطلب ووقته وعنوان الإنترنت لتشغيل الخدمة وحمايتها وتحليل استخدامها. وقد يستنتج موقعًا تقريبيًا من العنوان لتحسين الخدمة والامتثال للمتطلبات الضريبية. يستخدم ExchangeRate-API عنوان الإنترنت لتقييد عدد الطلبات مؤقتًا وحماية خدمته. يمكن ربط نشاط الطلب والموقع التقريبي بعنوان الإنترنت. لا يستخدم التطبيق هذه البيانات للتتبّع الإعلاني. سياسة Gold API · خدمة أسعار الصرف · شروط المزود

Gold for Gold · Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Hamam A. Alabdulla develops this app to estimate the value of gold and silver and calculate zakat. You can use it without an account.

Weights and calculations on your device

Piece weights, assays, quotes, holdings and zakat results stay in app memory for the session. Your language, currency, treatment of worn jewellery and other preferences are saved locally, along with the most recent rates. For each metal and currency, the app also saves the last displayed market price, the time you checked it and its source dates for comparison on your next check. Deleting the app from your device removes its local data.

When prices refresh

The app uses encrypted connections to request gold and silver prices from Gold API and USD-based exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API. Both receive the originating IP address. Requests contain no selected currency, weights, holdings, results or device identifier.

Gold API may log request paths, times and IP addresses to operate and secure the service and analyse usage. It may infer approximate location from the IP address to optimise the service and comply with tax requirements. ExchangeRate-API uses the address to temporarily limit requests and prevent abuse. Request activity and approximate location can be associated with the originating IP. The app does not use these data for advertising or tracking. Processing and retention

Calculations provide the service you request. Providers may process request data for their legitimate interests in operating and securing the service and to meet applicable legal obligations. Processing may take place outside your country. Each provider controls retention of its logs under its policy. The developer keeps no central record of your holdings. If you opt to share app diagnostics with Apple, Apple may make crash reports available to the developer according to your settings.

Your rights and contact

You can contact us to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data under applicable law. For provider logs, consult the provider’s policy as well. The app is for a general audience and does not request personal information from children. We will publish changes on this page with an updated date. Contact hamam@gccapps.com.
